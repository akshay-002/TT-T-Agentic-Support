# Roaming and travel passes

## Fictional travel product
TT&T offers a simulated travel day pass for $10.00 per used travel day in Exampleland. This is an invented destination for fixtures and does not establish service availability or pricing for any real country. International calling and roaming are different services; ask which the customer means when ambiguous.

## Billing
The invoice records one roaming-pass line item per billed use in this dataset. A travel event in account history identifies the underlying simulated day. A pass is additional to recurring plan charges and does not automatically change the base subscription. Use billing tools to confirm an actual charge.

## Recommendations
For a real destination not represented in the approved TT&T corpus, state that coverage and pricing are unavailable and offer support. Do not recommend a pass by extrapolating from Exampleland. Activating roaming products is outside the two-day MVP; the chatbot cannot enroll the customer or guarantee coverage.
