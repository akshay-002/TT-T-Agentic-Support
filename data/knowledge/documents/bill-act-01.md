# Activation fees

## Activation charge
TT&T charges a one-time activation fee of $35.00 for each new mobile line in this fictional product. An activation fee may be posted on the activation-month invoice or the following monthly invoice. Posting a fee later does not mean the line was activated twice. Use the activation record and the specific invoice item to identify the event.

## Explaining a bill
State the fee amount, affected masked line, activation date, and invoice where it appears. Separate the one-time fee from recurring service. Never infer a fee from the change in total alone. Account facts must come from authorized billing tools.

## Disputes
If a customer disputes the recorded activation or believes the charge is duplicated, offer a support ticket. The assistant cannot waive fees or issue refunds. A missing event reference requires review; it is not evidence that the fee is valid. Historical fixture lines without an activation order must not be assigned new fees by inference.
