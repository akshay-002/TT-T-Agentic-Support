# Calls SMS and Wi-Fi calling

## Calls and messages
Ask whether the issue affects outgoing calls, incoming calls, SMS, or all services. Confirm signal availability and whether airplane mode is off. Review the relevant voice or SMS outage report when available. A data-only incident should not be described as confirmed voice failure without evidence.

## Wi-Fi calling
Explain that Wi-Fi calling requires a supported device and an enabled service setting. For emergency-location configuration or device-specific changes, direct the customer to the approved secure settings workflow. Do not fabricate device menu paths that are absent from the knowledge base.

## Handoff
If the issue persists, offer a support ticket. The chatbot cannot route emergency calls or provide emergency response. For an immediate emergency, the fixed safety response should direct the user to contact local emergency services through an available phone or other appropriate channel. General emergency advice is not a reason to execute account tools.
