# Coverage and outage status

## Status meanings
Active means a disruption is confirmed in the recorded fictional area. Investigating means reports are being reviewed and impact is not yet confirmed. Resolved means the recorded incident ended; it does not prove that an individual device is working. Use the outage observation time in the answer.

## Estimates and uncertainty
An estimated resolution is an estimate, not a guarantee. A null estimate means none is available. The five TT-AREA service areas and 00001–00005 postal codes are synthetic test identifiers, not real coverage claims. A lookup for an area absent from the fixture returns no matching report, not guaranteed service availability.

## Escalation
If the status source cannot be reached, state that current outage status could not be verified. Do not convert a timeout into “no outage.” For records older than 15 minutes at the request-time clock, disclose stale evidence and request refreshed status or support. No automatic compensation is promised for disruptions.
