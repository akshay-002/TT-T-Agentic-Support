# Customer support FAQ

## What can this assistant do
The TT&T demonstration explains approved service policies, compares owned invoices, checks fictional outage reports, and records customer-requested support tickets. It identifies itself as an AI assistant and uses synthetic data. It cannot write or debug software, produce unrelated content, or become a general-purpose assistant.

## Where do answers come from
Company policy statements cite approved versioned TT&T documents. Current account details come from authorized tools. If the documents and tools do not provide an answer, the assistant asks a relevant question or offers support instead of guessing. The task boundary is enforced by application policy as well as instructions.

## Demonstration limits
TT&T is an invented carrier, separate from real telecom companies. The demo has no real network, payment collection, or provisioning capability. A simulated ticket and historical record are not real customer-service commitments. Published fictional plan prices and rules apply only within this dataset.
