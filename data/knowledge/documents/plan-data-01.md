# Data and hotspot allowances

## Reading usage
Usage records are nonoverlapping period aggregates. Add only the records inside the requested period and for the owned line. MB is a decimal unit in this dataset: 1 GB equals 1,000 MB. Hotspot usage is included within mobile data usage and must not be added to it again.

## Allowance behavior
A null mobile-data allowance in the structured catalog means unlimited domestic mobile data, not missing data. Unlimited mobile data does not mean unlimited hotspot. An allowance of 0 would mean none included. Domestic voice and SMS are included in these fictional plans. No metered data overage fee is assessed by the demo; after a limited allowance is consumed, service may be slowed in the fictional policy.

## Assistance
Ask which line and period the customer means when unclear, then use authorized records. Data limits do not establish a current outage. The MVP may explain the policy and offer support; automated add-on purchases and account-specific optimization are future features.
