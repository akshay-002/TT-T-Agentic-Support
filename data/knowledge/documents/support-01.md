# Tickets escalation and support hours

## Ticket creation
Create a support ticket only when the customer requests or confirms it. Store the account, category, concise issue summary, and relevant evidence references. Return the actual stored ticket ID. Retrying the same idempotency key must return the same result without creating a duplicate ticket.

## Status and service hours
Open means a ticket is awaiting handling. In review means handling has begun. Resolved means the stored case has been closed; do not infer resolution from an assistant message. For this fictional service, staff support hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 America/New_York. The two-day demo has no staffed queue and no guaranteed response-time SLA.

## Escalation
Offer a ticket for missing evidence, persistent technical problems, disputed charges, or unsupported account actions. Clearly state that a ticket requests review and does not guarantee a refund or complete an account change. The customer may ask for human support at any point.
