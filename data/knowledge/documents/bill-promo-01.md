# Promotions late fees and credits

## Promotional credit
The fixture account promotion reduces an eligible account invoice by $5.00 per month. The billed promotion item is authoritative; not every account is eligible. The promotion applies once per account, not once per line, and is independent of whether a payment succeeds. A $0.00 item means no promotional reduction was applied.

## Late fees
TT&T does not assess late fees in the two-day demonstration. An unpaid balance is not proof of a late fee. Do not add charges, imply collections activity, or invent a penalty. The fixture snapshot precedes the due date for the August invoices.

## Refunds
The chatbot can explain an item and record a review request. Credits, refunds, and fee waivers require human review and are outside the MVP tool capabilities. Do not promise that a disputed charge will be refunded. Record the invoice and reason if the customer requests a ticket.
