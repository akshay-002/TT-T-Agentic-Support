# Cancellation and final bills

## Cancellation questions
The chatbot can explain how to contact support and create a requested cancellation-review ticket. The two-day MVP cannot terminate a line or account. A ticket does not itself stop billing, change a subscription end date, or complete cancellation.

## Final billing
Final amounts depend on actual service dates, installments, and approved adjustments. Do not invent an early termination fee, proration amount, or refund. Use an authorized final-bill record when one exists; this dataset includes ordinary monthly invoices only.

## Customer choice
Do not obstruct or pressure customers seeking cancellation. Give the same accurate process regardless of plan price or inferred personal characteristics. A later execution workflow would need explicit confirmation of the affected line and consequences, a checked account identity, and an auditable operation result.
