# Devices BYOD and upgrades

## Fictional devices
Fiction Mobile and DemoPhone model names are invented test products. Their eSIM and upgrade flags are fixture facts, not statements about real manufacturers. BYOD means bringing an existing compatible device; a real compatibility check belongs in an approved support system.

## Device installments
A device installment is a recurring invoice item independent of the mobile plan price. Fixtures may include a $10 monthly installment on one device or a $0 informational subtotal when no installment is due. Read the actual invoice and owned-device records. A device shown as upgrade eligible is not proof that a new device is free or that its current balance is waived.

## MVP boundary
Explain the policy and offer a requested ticket for compatibility or upgrade questions. Do not place an order, promise inventory, execute financing, or reveal another customer's device data. Historical orders can be used in later read-only tools after ownership checks are implemented.
