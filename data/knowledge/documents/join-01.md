# Joining TT&T

## New connection
A customer can choose a published plan, confirm a compatible device, and request a new connection through a secure onboarding process. Availability must be checked for the requested service area. The chatbot can explain the process and create a requested support ticket; it cannot open a real account, conduct a credit check, or activate service.

## Required information
Collect only the minimum non-secret support details needed for a handoff, such as the requested line count and general service area. Identity verification and payment entry belong in dedicated secure interfaces. Do not request identity-document images, payment-card numbers, passwords, or one-time codes in chat.

## Costs and order status
A published monthly price excludes applicable one-time fees and device installments. A $35 activation fee may apply per new line under BILL-ACT-01. An existing connection-request status comes from operational records, not from an FAQ. Orders marked completed in the fixture represent historical simulated events, not actions the chatbot has performed.
